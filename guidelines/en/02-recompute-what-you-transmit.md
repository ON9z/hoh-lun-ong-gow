# Restating requires recomputing: any number or root cause quoted from someone else (or from your own previous round) must be computed on the spot

判据：Is this number / root cause **something I computed**, or **something I restated**? If restated, is it explicitly marked "unverified"?

**Instances (same shape ≥4 times in one day)**:
- restated an effect figure of "320→244"; the measured value was "811→720, **with a two-way sign flip**";
- restated "a certain metric cannot be read"; measured, it **could** be read;
- passed "a certain writing convention" to the next subagent as the root cause; **the real root cause was a different one**;
- copied its **own previous round's** conclusion into the single ledger, and **found only in the next round that the conclusion was wrong**.

**⇒ Why it is dangerous**: the error propagates **in a tone of authority**, and the recipient **has no reason to doubt it**.
**⇒ Practice**: if you cannot compute it ⇒ write "restated from X, not independently verified" explicitly in the entry.
**Label every "root cause" handed downstream as a lead, not a conclusion** — especially judgments **about the other party's behavior**, such as "Y happened because you used X at the time".
